THE CLASSIC COLLECTION

A classic, under control.

The CLA-3A brings the character of a vintage optical compressor to your browser.

01 / Shape the signal

Drag Gain and Peak Reduction up or down. Hold Shift for finer adjustments. Double-click any knob to restore its default.

02 / Find your balance

Use Mix for parallel compression and Trim to balance the output. COMP gives gentle leveling; LIMITER applies a firmer ratio.

03 / Compare & save

Switch between Setup A and B to compare settings. A→B copies the current slot. Save stores your preset in this browser.

04 / Listen

Play a synthesized demo and adjust the compressor. Bypass compares the dry signal. VU Display selects input, gain reduction, or output metering.

Interface recreation with a browser audio demonstration. The demo uses Web Audio compression, not an emulation of Waves DSP.
